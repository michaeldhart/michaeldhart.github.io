Hi, I'm Michael. I'm a software engineer, dad, arts supporter, and local troublemaker.

I'm a Belvidere native (BHS class of 1998) with a bachelor's degree in music education from Illinois Wesleyan University. I spent six years in New York founding and working with nonprofit organizations, then returned to Belvidere in 2010. I briefly taught with D100 before moving into software engineering.

I've been a Boone County Arts Council board member for more than 12 years and serve as Vice Chair of the Boone County Democrats, and I volunteer in leadership roles with other community organizations. I also make videos about local politics.

I live in Belvidere with my wife Lauren, three daughters, two dogs, and a cat.

We have an opportunity now to imagine new systems. We have an opportunity to build more resilient communities.

You can reach me at [michael@michaeldhart.com](mailto:michael@michaeldhart.com).
